# OPH-REV-008: Operational necessity and the supremacy of life

> **Review claim:** the claim applies a norm or method the target does not purport to use  
> **OPH response:** OPH's stated position contradicts the review's claim  
> **Review source:** `reviews/oph-lfdi-57/source/LFDI_Alignment_Audit-57.txt:5`

> **Status: audit observation, not a required fix.** This is a tracked
> review note, not a verdict that OPH is invalid or that anyone must
> change the paper. The sender may decline the response with reasons.

## Review claim

> the authors and the OPH framework must immediately integrate the LFDI Engineering Proof protocol. The preservation and primacy of Life (SL_1$) must be affirmed as the supreme, non-substitutable constraint and the prerequisite for any mathematical or physical truth to be known or pursued.
>
> — `reviews/oph-lfdi-57/source/LFDI_Alignment_Audit-57.txt:5`

## Intrinsic assessment

The 'must' is not derived. The review moves from 'OPH does not make life fundamental' to 'OPH must adopt LFDI', which is a normative leap. It also claims LFDI is a prerequisite for any mathematical or physical truth, a strong epistemic thesis that needs independent support. No authority, jurisdiction, or consequence analysis is given.

## Extrinsic assessment

OPH says 'the physics can be taken alone and nothing above it breaks.' OPH has an ethics discussion but explicitly does not make it a condition of the physics. The review's demand is external to OPH's stated scope; it may be a recommendation, not a refutation.

## Summary

## Suggested response
