# OPH-REV-008: Operational necessity and the supremacy of life

> **Review claim:** the claim applies a norm or method the target does not purport to use  
> **OPH response:** OPH's stated position contradicts the review's claim; a contradiction is not by itself a refutation  
> **Review source:** `reviews/oph-lfdi-57/source/LFDI_Alignment_Audit-57.verified.txt:5`

> **Status: audit observation, not a required fix.** This is a tracked
> review note, not a verdict that OPH is invalid or that anyone must
> change the paper. The sender may decline the response with reasons.

## Review claim

> the authors and the OPH framework must immediately integrate the LFDI Engineering Proof protocol. The preservation and primacy of Life ($L_1$) must be affirmed as the supreme, non-substitutable constraint and the prerequisite for any mathematical or physical truth to be known or pursued.
>
> — `reviews/oph-lfdi-57/source/LFDI_Alignment_Audit-57.verified.txt:5`

## Intrinsic assessment

The 'must' is not derived. The review moves from the observation that OPH does not make life fundamental to the demand that OPH adopt LFDI, which is a normative leap. It also claims LFDI is a prerequisite for any mathematical or physical truth, a strong epistemic thesis that needs independent support. WISLIM's LFDI is narrower: a guard on actions that affect life, with an explicit else-pass branch (source/WISLIM-v2.0.1.txt:212). No authority, jurisdiction, or consequence analysis is given.

## Extrinsic assessment

Asked 'Do I have to accept the ethics to accept the physics?', OPH answers 'the physics can be taken alone and nothing above it breaks'. The same passage says 'They share a quantity' and 'Accepting the repair law is accepting both'. OPH therefore does not make an ethics a condition of its physics, and it does not treat the two as unrelated either. An earlier version of this record said OPH 'explicitly separates physics from ethics', which overstated the passage (OPH-AUD-003). The review's demand is external to OPH's stated scope; it may be a recommendation, not a refutation.

## Summary

A normative demand is issued without an argument. OPH says a reader can take its physics without its ethics, and also that the ethics reads the same quantity as the physics. It does not make LFDI a condition of either.

## Suggested response

Separate the proposal that OPH should adopt an ethics from the claim that OPH's physics is false. Argue the former; do not assert the latter.
