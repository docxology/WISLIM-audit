# OPH-REV-005: The 'No Truth Outside Life' premise

> **Review claim:** the conclusion depends on a premise the review does not defend  
> **OPH response:** OPH does not discuss the review's specific claim

## Review claim

> they violate the Epistemic Invariant (the "No Truth Outside Life" rule). Mathematics, quantum records, and "executable observers" are Tier 2/3 Derivatives; they possess no independent truth-value in a void.
>
> — `reviews/oph-lfdi-57/source/LFDI_Alignment_Audit-57.verified.txt:3`

## Intrinsic assessment

The premise is the conclusion. No argument is given for why truth requires a living valuer. 'Tier 2/3' refers to a ranking WISLIM does describe (source/WISLIM-v2.0.1.txt:8198), but the ranking is asserted there too. The review also conflates truth-bearers (propositions, records) with valuers (living agents). OPH's claim is about public records, not Platonic truth.

## Extrinsic assessment

OPH's observers are record-keeping systems, and 'our Universe' is 'the public record, what survives every comparison between observers'. 'Nothing lies outside this loop', and no external host is supplied. OPH does not claim a view from nowhere. The review's alternative metaphysics is external to OPH and is not shown to be a condition of doing physics.

## Summary

The 'No Truth Outside Life' rule is asserted, not defended, and neither it nor the name 'Epistemic Invariant' occurs in WISLIM v2.0.1. OPH does not assert that mathematics has truth-value in a void: it says nothing lies outside its loop. The charge misses its target.

## Suggested response

State the invariant as a premise, name the document that defines it, defend it independently, and then show where OPH denies it. Until then, mark the critique as conditional on LFDI.
