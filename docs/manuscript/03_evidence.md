A named result in mathematics or science can motivate an analogy. The audit
asks whether the example also supplies premises connecting that result to
the proposed ethical rule. It classifies three examples as valid but
insufficient because their valid point leaves the paper’s larger conclusion
open [@wislim_audit].

### The AI letters

Appendix B includes published letters attributed to seven models. One letter
describes a shared workspace, a clarification of the term “life,” and an
elicitation framed around whether the thesis was among the important truths
or the most important one [@wishengrad2026] (`source/WISLIM-v2.0.1.txt:1036–1038, 1088–1092, and 1114–1120`). Another passage reports “100% consensus”
[@wishengrad2026] (`source/WISLIM-v2.0.1.txt:6853–6855`). These statements establish what the
publication reports.

For the reported convergence to support independent agreement, the audit
would need the proposition supplied to each model, the sampling and selection
record, and evidence that the responses were elicited independently.
The archived publication does not supply the complete prompts and session
records. The audit therefore treats shared context and selection as competing
explanations whose effects cannot be estimated from the letters alone
[@wislim_audit].
