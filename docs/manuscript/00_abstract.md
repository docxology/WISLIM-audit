## Abstract

The WISLIM paper argues that because life is a precondition for experience
and valuation, protecting life should outrank every other objective whenever
life is affected [@wishengrad2026] (`source/WISLIM-v2.0.1.txt:148–154`). This report summarizes
an independent textual audit of version 2.0.1. It separates the premise
about the conditions for valuation from the proposed decision rule and asks
whether the paper’s definitions, formula, stress-test catalogue, and
published AI letters bridge that gap. The audit finds that they leave the
normative premise and the evidentiary independence of the letters
unestablished. A narrower claim about safeguards against catastrophic or
irreversible harm remains open to argument. The report draws on the
repository’s versioned audit and evidence ledgers [@wislim_audit].
