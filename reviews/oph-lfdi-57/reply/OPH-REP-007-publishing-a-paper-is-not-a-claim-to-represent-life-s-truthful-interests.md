# OPH-REP-007: Publishing a paper is not a claim to represent 'life's truthful interests'

> **Status: audit observation, not a required fix.** This is a tracked note on one
> claim in a reply. It is not a verdict on anyone's character, and the sender may
> decline the suggested step with reasons. Comments on the matching issue are welcome.

## The reply says

> The very existence of your paper and you publishing it is claiming to represent life's truthful interests.
>
> — [`reviews/oph-lfdi-57/correspondence/03-wishengrad-reply.txt:39`](https://github.com/docxology/WISLIM-audit/blob/main/reviews/oph-lfdi-57/correspondence/03-wishengrad-reply.txt#L39)

## Summary

No quotation is offered. 'Truthful interests' occurs in neither OPH nor WISLIM v2.0.1. The frame 'claiming to represent life' is WISLIM's own, applied to authors who did not use it.

## Assessment

A search of every text file at the pinned OPH commit, allowing for line wraps, finds no 'truthful interest', 'represent life', or 'interests of life'. The book does use 'truthfully', of which continuations can hold an offender, not of anyone's interests. WISLIM v2.0.1 has no 'truthful interest' either. The nearest source is WISLIM's closing, which says that anything less than affirmation 'for a person or an organization claiming to represent life is the very betrayal of all life and truth'.

A physics manuscript makes claims about physics. Reading a role into the act of publishing, and then judging the author by that role, supplies the premise that the next sentence needs (OPH-REP-008).

The reply says 'I'm not out to duel with you', and that is welcome. The response's author says the same: he is not out to duel anyone, and he contributes at the level and quality of modularity and integrity the seriousness of the situation calls for. The sentence above interprets his collaborative acts and attributes claims to him with no quotation.

**Kind of claim:** `attribution`: attributes to the response or its author something the text does not say.
