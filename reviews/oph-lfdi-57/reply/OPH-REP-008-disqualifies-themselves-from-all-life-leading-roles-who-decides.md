# OPH-REP-008: 'Disqualifies themselves from all life leading roles': who decides?

> **Status: audit observation, not a required fix.** This is a tracked note on one
> claim in a reply. It is not a verdict on anyone's character, and the sender may
> decline the suggested step with reasons. Comments on the matching issue are welcome.

## The reply says

> From my perspective, anyone claiming to represent life's truthful interests who has been shared the Truth that is the cure who is not sharing a higher truth to justify not sharing it or properly showing it is wrong or agreeing and sharing, disqualifies themselves from all life leading roles. Those are your options.
>
> — [`reviews/oph-lfdi-57/correspondence/03-wishengrad-reply.txt:40`](https://github.com/docxology/WISLIM-audit/blob/main/reviews/oph-lfdi-57/correspondence/03-wishengrad-reply.txt#L40)

## Summary

The sentence sets the terms and the penalty from one perspective, and names no procedure or judge. 'Properly' and 'life leading roles' are undefined. The audit is an attempt at the option the sentence offers, and WISLIM's own bright line lists five routes for it.

## Assessment

This is WISLIM's Integrity Screen, widened. There the third response is 'operationally disqualifying' in 'life-affecting contexts'. Here the penalty reaches 'all life leading roles', and no role is named.

'Properly showing it is wrong' is a real option in the sentence, and WISLIM's section 'What counts as a legitimate challenge (bright line)' says what it could mean: refute the operational definition, provide a superior one, propose a more important coordination truth, demonstrate a robust failure mode, or prove internal incoherence. The 66 observations in this repository are an attempt at that option. They bear on routes 1, 4, and 5: WISLIM-006 and WISLIM-035 on the definition, WISLIM-045 to WISLIM-047 as failure scenarios, and WISLIM-029 on agency and life. They are observations, not proofs, and whether any of them succeeds is open. The register invites reply.

The disqualification therefore applies only after someone judges that the attempt was not 'proper', and the sentence does not say who judges, by what procedure, or with what appeal. The operative question is the one Carroll gave Humpty Dumpty: 'which is to be master—that’s all.'

**Kind of claim:** `framing`: defines in advance what counts as an answer.
