## Summary

These sentences move from the argument to a motive and an office. The burden of proof normally rests with the party making the claim, 'fix your paper' names no defect, and no role is named to step down from. 'The cure' is the disputed claim, stated as a fact.

## Assessment

'Fix your paper' names no theorem, sentence, or Lean declaration (OPH-REV-010). 'Ego' attributes a motive with no evidence and offers a false choice between agreeing and vanity. 'The audit stands' is an assertion, and 'The goal of the LFDI is not to attack your physics' sits oddly beside it, since the audit's verdict was 'terminal failure'.

The burden lies with the party asserting that one framework is the cure and is binding on others. The claim that the framework cures all needless suffering is tracked at WISLIM-005 and WISLIM-021. Silence as betrayal is tracked at WISLIM-024. The review's demand was answered before this reply arrived: the 66-observation register was published and linked in the first response, and it is an attempt to disprove, which is one of the two things this sentence asks for.

LFDI's own rule is 'No Deception or Coercion When Life Is Affected', and WISLIM-014 asks that the framework apply that rule to itself. A demand that a named person leave unnamed roles unless they rank a claim first is worth reading against that rule.

**Kind of claim:** `demand`: moves from argument to motive, burden, or office.
