# OPH-REV-005: The 'No Truth Outside Life' premise

> **Review claim:** the conclusion depends on a premise the review does not defend  
> **OPH response:** OPH does not discuss the review's specific claim  
> **Review source:** `reviews/oph-lfdi-57/source/LFDI_Alignment_Audit-57.txt:3`

## Review claim

> they violate the Epistemic Invariant (the "No Truth Outside Life" rule). Mathematics, quantum records, and "executable observers" are Tier 2/3 Derivatives; they possess no independent truth-value in a void.
>
> — `reviews/oph-lfdi-57/source/LFDI_Alignment_Audit-57.txt:3`

## Intrinsic assessment

The premise is the conclusion. No argument is given for why truth requires a living valuer, nor what 'Tier 2/3' means. The review also conflates truth-bearers (propositions, records) with valuers (living agents). OPH's claim is about public records, not Platonic truth.

## Extrinsic assessment

OPH's observers are record-keeping systems; facts become public through overlap and repair. OPH does not claim a view from nowhere, nor that mathematics floats free of observers. The review's alternative metaphysics is external to OPH and is not shown to be a condition of doing physics.

## Summary

The 'No Truth Outside Life' rule and the Tier 2/3 hierarchy are asserted, not defended. OPH does not assert that mathematics has independent truth-value in a void, so the charge misses its target.

## Suggested response

State the invariant as a premise, defend it independently, and then show where OPH denies it. Until then, mark the critique as conditional on LFDI.
