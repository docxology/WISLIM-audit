## Review claim

> The authors commit a terminal Performative Contradiction by utilizing their living decision-making agency ($L_1$) to author, compute, and argue for a framework that subordinates Life to a mathematical abstraction.
>
> — `reviews/oph-lfdi-57/source/LFDI_Alignment_Audit-57.verified.txt:3`

## Intrinsic assessment

This is a genetic and use-mention confusion. The act of authoring is a fact about the authors, not a premise in OPH's formalism. A contradiction would require showing that OPH's premises entail 'no living authors' or that life is metaphysically fundamental, and that the authors affirm both. The review does neither.

## Extrinsic assessment

OPH's closure hypothesis says observers 'build hardware that realizes that same history, including its builders', and 'Nothing lies outside this loop'. Its working terms require 'The builders' inference' to be among the 'operations available within the candidate history'. OPH does not claim that life is unreal or that living authors cannot do physics, and nothing in it subordinates life to an abstraction. The review's target is a position OPH does not hold.

## Summary

Using living agency to write a theory does not contradict a theory that says physical observers need not be alive. OPH's closure hypothesis places its builders inside the structure it describes. The review asserts a contradiction without formalizing it.

## Suggested response

Ask for a formal derivation: premises, inference, and the exact contradiction. Otherwise withdraw 'terminal' and present the point as a framework-relative objection.
