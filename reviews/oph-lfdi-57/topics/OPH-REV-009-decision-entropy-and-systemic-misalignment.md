## Intrinsic assessment

No metric, baseline, or causal mechanism is given. The symbol $D$ is called 'severe epistemic distortion' earlier in the review and 'Decision Entropy' here; WISLIM v2.0.1 calls it 'delay, distortion, or destruction' and 'ethical entropy (D)' (source/WISLIM-v2.0.1.txt:441, :481). The term 'Decision Entropy' does not occur in WISLIM v2.0.1. The DOI is a citation, not evidence of necessity. The claim is speculative and unfalsifiable as stated. An earlier version of this record read $D$ as an acronym 'SDS' and called that an equivocation; 'SDS' was an OCR misreading (OPH-AUD-001). The shift in what $D$ names is real; the acronym was not.

## Extrinsic assessment

OPH's ethics is about mismatch and repair; the review does not show that OPH increases either. OPH's claims are explicitly conditional and carry a stated falsifier ('A frozen dispersion band lets one resolved measurement exclude the entire carrier class'); the review does not engage them. The causal claim is not addressed by OPH and is not verifiable from the review.
