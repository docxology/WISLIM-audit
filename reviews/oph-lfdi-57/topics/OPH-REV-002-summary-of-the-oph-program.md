## Review claim

> It attempts to derive standard physics (quantum fields, gravity, spacetime) from a "timeless self-referential history"
>
> — `reviews/oph-lfdi-57/source/LFDI_Alignment_Audit-57.verified.txt:2`

## Intrinsic assessment

The review accurately reports the observer-first and timeless-self-reference framing, and at release r2060 its phrases 'timeless self-referential history', 'operational observers' and 'twelve-port icosahedral carrier' occur in the paper. 'Attempts' is fair. It does not report the paper's status: 'treating the universe as an executable mathematical model' omits that this is the executable-model hypothesis, not an established result.

## Extrinsic assessment

The r2060 abstract says 'Measured by the four conditions, the program is incomplete' and 'Closure of one complete history is a hypothesis that the model tests'. The review's summary is directionally right but not calibrated to that status. The r2061 text puts the limit differently: 'the common physical $3+1$ limit is the remaining conclusion to be demonstrated', and the identification with our universe 'requires a common interacting chiral $3+1$-dimensional limit with the observed particle spectrum, responsive geometry and independent empirical agreement'.
